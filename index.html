        The Wispr Line

        How a girl with a very fast brain found a way to keep up with it. All aboard!

        1. 1

          Departure

          The Thought Tornado

          My brain runs really quickly. Ideas pile up faster than my fingers can type them, and when I say them out loud they come out full of “um”, “like” and “so basically”.

          I wanted to note down every single thing I was thinking, but I had no idea how.

        2. 2

          Signal Tower

          Product Space's Agentic AI Hackathon

          At one point I had never even heard of Wispr Flow. Then I heard about the Agentic AI Hackathon by Product Space and signed up.

          That's where Wispr Flow first came onto my track.

        3. 3

          First Words Junction

          “Wait… that's it?”

          I started using it while I was building, and it was really easy. I just talked, and clean text showed up wherever my cursor was.

          No setup drama, no learning curve. I simply kept using it.

        4. 4

          Wispr Works Yard

          A hackathon built only on Wispr Flow

          Then I joined another hackathon where every project was a Wispr Flow project.

          This time Flow wasn't just a tool I used. It was the thing I was building on top of.

        5. 5

          Main Line · Laptop HQ

          Where Flow really lives

          I use Wispr Flow most on my laptop. I have a lot of thoughts, and I need to be able to say them coherently. So I just talk — filler words and all — and Flow notes down everything I mean, cleaned up.

          💻 Laptopmost of the time

          The kind of ramble I said while planning this site:

          So um, basically, I want like, every island on the map to have its own page, you know, and uh, wait, also a section about how I um, found Wispr Flow, like through the hackathon and stuff.

          I want every island on the map to have its own page, plus a section about how I found Wispr Flow through the hackathon.

        6. 6

          Side Halt · Phone

          A quieter branch line

          I use it a little on my phone too, every now and then. My laptop is still where most of my thinking out loud happens.

        7. ★

          Next Stop

          Wispr Flow · Growth

          I'm the user Flow was made for: a fast brain, lots of filler words, and a laptop full of half-written ideas. I've lived the “wait, that's it?” moment myself.

          I also spend my days with students and first-time founders — as CEO of Catalyst IEDC, mentoring students from 20+ Kerala schools through SALT, and on stage as an anchor. Those rooms are exactly where tools spread by word of mouth.

          Ideas I'd love to try

          • Campus “talk, don't type” demo days through IEDC and startup-cell networks.
          • Hackathon tracks built on Flow, like the one I joined, to turn users into builders.
          • Show the “works in every app” moment in the very first minute.
          • Manglish rambles → clean English: a fun way to show how people in Kerala really talk.
